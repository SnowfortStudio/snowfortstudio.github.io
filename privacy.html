Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

CrownShot ("the game") is made by Snowfort Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game shows ads. Ad partners use information about your device to show ads and measure them. They can only use your device's advertising identifier if you allow it when iOS asks ("Allow tracking?").
  • Game statistics are sent only if you accept them, and you can turn them off at any time (OPTIONS → ANALYTICS).
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, crowns, upgrades, outfits, settings) is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it. We have no account system and no server that stores your game.

2. Game statistics (only if you accept)

The first time you open the game, it asks whether you agree to share anonymous statistics. If you accept, the game sends:

  • Gameplay events: levels started, won or lost, stars earned, upgrades, power cards and outfits chosen, time played, ads watched and purchases made in the game;
  • Technical information: device model, operating system version, game version, language, frame rate, error and crash reports, approximate country (worked out from your internet connection), and a random identifier created for the game.

Why: to find levels that are too easy or too hard, fix problems and improve the game.

Who: GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark) processes these statistics for us. Their privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

If you decline, these statistics are not sent. You can still play everything.

3. Ads

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks connected to it (currently Unity Ads, Google AdMob and Mintegral). LevelPlay and Unity Ads are operated by Unity Technologies and its affiliates (formerly ironSource), AdMob by Google LLC, and Mintegral by Mintegral International Limited. To choose, deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • device identifiers: the iOS identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size, connection type) and your approximate location worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks, rewards) and with the game.

If you allow tracking, ad partners may use this information to show ads that match your interests across apps from other companies. If you do not, they still show ads, without using the advertising identifier.

You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Unity's privacy policy for players: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. Google: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Mintegral: mintegral.com/en/privacy.

The NO ADS purchase removes the ads that start on their own and gives you the ad rewards for free.

4. In-game purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under your Apple account. Apple tells the game that a purchase succeeded so we can deliver it (and restore it if you reinstall the game). We never receive your name, address or payment card. Purchase handling uses Unity's in-app purchase service. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not combine it with other information about you.
  • The game has no chat, no user profiles and no social features.

6. How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete the game. Statistics and ad data are kept by GameAnalytics and Unity according to their own retention rules, only as long as needed for the purposes above.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example Québec, the rest of Canada, the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to withdraw your consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to us at the address below: we answer within 30 days. The game has no accounts, so we may need your help to find the data linked to your device; you can also contact GameAnalytics or Unity directly through the links above. Turning off ANALYTICS in the game, or tracking in iOS Settings, withdraws your consent at any time.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
