Frequently asked questions

I bought NO ADS and it disappeared.

Open SHOP, go to the NO ADS tab and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple account you bought it with. The crowns, the Royal Cape and the baby dragon come back with it.

My progress is gone.

Your progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it, and it does not move to a new device.

How do I stop sharing statistics?

In the game, open OPTIONS and turn off ANALYTICS.

How do I limit ad tracking?

On your iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn tracking off for CrownShot. You will still see ads, but they will not use your advertising identifier.

Is the game available in my language?

The game is in English for now.
